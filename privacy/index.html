PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 15, 2026

A Good Walk, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the A Good Walk mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information: Name and email address, username and profile photo, account credentials (managed securely by Auth0).

Golf Experience Data: Golf courses played and dates, scorecards and round statistics, photos and videos you upload, experience notes and descriptions, playing partners and group information.

Location Information: Location data to help you find nearby golf courses, course locations associated with your rounds.

Device Information: Device identifiers for push notifications, device type and operating system.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to: provide and maintain the app's core functionality, enable you to track and memorialize your golf experiences, connect you with friends and other golfers, send push notifications about friend requests, invitations, and app updates, generate AI-powered summaries and suggestions for your experiences, and improve and personalize your experience.

INFORMATION SHARING

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

Other Users: Based on your privacy settings (Private, Friends, or Public), other users may see your profile, experiences, and golf activity.

Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services to operate our app including Auth0 for secure authentication, Google Maps for course location services, Firebase for media storage, and Expo for app updates and notifications.

DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information: all data transmitted using HTTPS encryption, authentication tokens stored securely on your device, passwords managed by Auth0 and never stored in plaintext.

YOUR PRIVACY CONTROLS

You can control your privacy through the app's Settings:

Profile Visibility: Choose Private, Friends Only, or Public.
Experience Visibility: Set visibility for each experience you create.
Friends: Manage friend connections and requests.
Media: Delete photos and videos at any time.

DATA RETENTION

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You may delete your experiences, rounds, and media at any time within the app. To request complete account deletion, please contact us.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
